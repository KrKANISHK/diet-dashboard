🌿 Anti-Bloating Essentials

  • Prefer well-cooked lauki, torai, zucchini, spinach and carrots.
  • Use yellow moong dal on vegetarian days; temper with hing + jeera.
  • Avoid large portions of rajma/chole and raw cruciferous salads, especially late.
  • Use ginger, ajwain and saunf in your evening/night routine.
  • Eat slowly and keep dinner lighter than lunch.
  • Target ~3L water/day, spread throughout the day.
