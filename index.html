🌿 Anti-Bloating Essentials

  • Prefer well-cooked lauki, torai, zucchini, spinach and carrots.
  • Tuesday/Saturday: yellow moong with hing + jeera; keep portions moderate.
  • Avoid large rajma/chole portions and heavy unfermented dals when bloated.
  • Avoid raw cruciferous salads late in the day.
  • Use ginger, ajwain and saunf in evening/night routines.
  • Eat slowly and keep dinner lighter than lunch.
  • Spread 2.5–3L water through the day.

🩸 Prediabetes + Fatty Liver

  • No added sugar, sweetened drinks, sweets, refined flour, biscuits or sugary desserts.
  • Use measured oats, whole-wheat roti, quinoa/millets or modest rice portions; pair starch with protein and vegetables.
  • Prefer grilled/air-fried/boiled chicken and eggs; avoid fried/processed foods and trans fats.
  • Measure mustard/olive oil; use flaxseed/walnuts in small portions.
  • Choose whole fruit rather than juice.
