🎯 Daily Targets & Rules

Calorie ceiling: 1,500 kcal/day. Protein 110–130g, carbs ~130–160g, fat ~45–55g, fibre ~25–30g, water ~3L/day. This is a relatively aggressive calorie target; if you feel weak, dizzy, unusually hungry, or your training suffers, increase intake and discuss the plan with a qualified clinician/dietitian.

For sensitive digestion: prefer cooked foods, moderate oil, smaller portions of legumes, and avoid forcing fibre upward when bloating is active.

🌿 Anti-Bloating Essentials

  • Prefer well-cooked lauki, torai, zucchini, spinach and carrots.
  • Tuesday/Saturday: yellow moong dal with hing + jeera rather than heavy lentils.
  • Avoid large portions of rajma/chole and heavy unfermented dals when bloating is active.
  • Avoid raw cruciferous salads, especially late in the day.
  • Use ginger, ajwain and saunf in the evening/night routine.
  • Eat slowly and keep dinner lighter than lunch.
  • Spread ~3L water across the day.

💊 Micronutrients

  • Prioritize micronutrients from vegetables, fruit, dairy/fortified alternatives, eggs/chicken and whole grains.
  • A multivitamin is not automatically necessary if the diet is adequate. Avoid high-dose supplements without clinician advice.
  • Vitamin D, B12, iron and other supplements are best guided by diet history and/or labs rather than guessed.
