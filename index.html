🌿 Anti-Bloating Essentials

  • Prioritize well-cooked lauki, torai, zucchini, spinach and carrots.
  • Vegetarian days: use yellow moong dal, tempered with hing + jeera.
  • Avoid heavy unfermented lentils and large rajma/chole portions when bloating is active.
  • Avoid raw cruciferous salads, especially late in the day.
  • Use ginger, ajwain and saunf in the evening/night routine.
  • Eat slowly, chew well and keep dinner lighter than lunch.
  • Spread ~3L water across the day rather than drinking very large amounts at once.
